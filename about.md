I'm **Jeremia Oktavian Chrisnanto**, a graduate student in the Laboratory of Protein Science at
Hokkaido University in Sapporo, Japan.

### What I work on

My research is about **antimicrobial peptides**: short, positively charged peptides that animals
make to kill bacteria. I study **AsCATH5**, a cathelicidin from the Chinese alligator, and ask how
it tells a bacterial membrane apart from one of ours.

I combine two ways of looking at it:

- **NMR spectroscopy**: measuring the peptide's structure in membrane-like micelles.
- **Molecular dynamics simulations**: following the peptide atom by atom as it binds to lipid
  bilayers.
