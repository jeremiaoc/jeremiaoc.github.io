Every PhD student eventually discovers that the lab notebook is a strange kind of writing. It is
careful, dated, full of numbers — and almost completely silent about what you were *thinking*.

This blog is for the other half: the thinking.

## What to expect

I'll write about four things:

1. **Research** — what I'm learning about peptides, membranes and simulations, and the mistakes
   that taught me the most.
2. **Science** — how we decide something is true, and how often we're wrong about that.
3. **Philosophy** — the questions underneath the questions.
4. **Random** — Sapporo winters, books, food, and whatever else.

> Writing is the way I find out what I actually think.

Posts will be irregular. Some will be long, most short. If something here is useful or wrong,
I'd like to hear about it.
